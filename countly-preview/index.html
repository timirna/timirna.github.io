Countly stays centred inside his artboard. In your app, his size and position are set by your code, the two triggers, wave and frustrate, play the reactions, setting loading to true plays the coffin loop, cooking plays the cauldron loop and scanning the food scan and fridge the fridge loop, each until you set it back to false. For the food scan, set the image property mealPhoto to the user's meal photo (4:3, ideally 800 × 560) and it appears in the frame he studies.
